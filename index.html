HeadCount – Privacy Policy

Effective date: 26 October 2026

HeadCount is an Android app that counts people in a photo and compares attendance between photos. It was developed by Sviatoslav Pokhvalenko as part of a bachelor's thesis. This policy explains what data the app processes and how.

Summary

All processing happens on your device. HeadCount sends nothing automatically and never sells data. A photo leaves the device only when you choose to share it, or to upload it to the research dataset (see "Optional dataset upload").

Data the app processes

  • Photos that you take with the camera or choose from the gallery. A copy of each saved photo is kept in the app's private storage.
  • Face data derived from those photos: the position of each detected face and a numeric face descriptor (a list of 128 numbers) used to recognise whether the same person appears in another photo. The descriptor cannot be used to identify a person by name.
  • Information you enter: names of photos and groups.
  • Results: the number of people and the dates of saved photos.

The app requests camera access, used by its built-in camera in panorama mode, and internet access, used only by the optional dataset upload. The face detection and recognition models are bundled with the app and run on the device. No account or registration is needed, and the app collects no contact details, location, device identifiers, usage analytics or advertising data.

Purpose

This data is used only to show you how many people are in a photo and, for photos in the same group, who is new, who is present and who is absent.

Storage, retention and deletion

Data is stored only in the app's private storage on your device and is not accessible to other apps. It stays there until you delete it. You can delete individual photos or whole groups in the app at any time; deleting a photo also deletes its face data. Uninstalling the app or clearing its storage removes all of its data.

Sharing

Apart from photos you upload to the research dataset, no data is shared with the developer or any third party. The app contains no advertising and no analytics or tracking libraries.

If you tap Share on a photo, the original photo is handed to the app you choose in Android's share menu (for example a cloud storage or messaging app). HeadCount itself does not send it anywhere; what happens next is governed by the chosen app.

Optional dataset upload

Each photo has an Upload to dataset button. Only when you tap it and confirm, that photo is uploaded to the HeadCount research dataset, an upload-only folder on the Nextcloud server of the Faculty of Information Technology, Brno University of Technology (FIT VUT), used by the developer to evaluate the app's accuracy in the bachelor's thesis. The photo is sent without location or other metadata and without any face data. Nothing is uploaded automatically. Please upload only photos whose people agreed to it. To have an uploaded photo removed, contact the developer.

Photos of other people

If you photograph other people, you are responsible for having their consent where the law requires it.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13.
