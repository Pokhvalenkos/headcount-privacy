HeadCount – Privacy Policy

Effective date: 12 October 2026

HeadCount is an Android app that counts people in a photo and compares attendance between photos. It was developed by Sviatoslav Pokhvalenko as part of a bachelor's thesis. This policy explains what data the app processes and how.

Summary

All processing happens on your device. HeadCount does not collect, upload, sell or share any data. The app does not request internet access, so it cannot send data anywhere.

Data the app processes

  • Photos that you take with the camera or choose from the gallery. A copy of each saved photo is kept in the app's private storage.
  • Face data derived from those photos: the position of each detected face and a numeric face descriptor (a list of 128 numbers) used to recognise whether the same person appears in another photo. The descriptor cannot be used to identify a person by name.
  • Information you enter: names of photos and folders.
  • Results: the number of people and the dates of saved photos.

The face detection and recognition models are bundled with the app and run on the device. No account or registration is needed, and the app collects no contact details, location, device identifiers, usage analytics or advertising data.

Purpose

This data is used only to show you how many people are in a photo and, for photos in the same folder, who is new, who came again and who is missing.

Storage, retention and deletion

Data is stored only in the app's private storage on your device and is not accessible to other apps. It stays there until you delete it. You can delete individual photos or whole folders in the app at any time; deleting a photo also deletes its face data. Uninstalling the app or clearing its storage removes all of its data.

Sharing

No data is shared with the developer or any third party. The app contains no advertising and no analytics or tracking libraries.

Photos of other people

If you photograph other people, you are responsible for having their consent where the law requires it.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13.
